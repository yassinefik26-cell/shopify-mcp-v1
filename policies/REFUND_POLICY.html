Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

At DRIPHOPE, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If you are not completely satisfied with your order, you may be eligible to return your item in accordance with the policy below.

1. 30-Day Return Policy

We offer a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days from the date you receive your order to request a return.

To be eligible for a return, your item must meet all of the following conditions:

  • The item must be in the same condition in which you received it.

  • The item must be unworn and unused.

  • The item must have its original tags attached.

  • The item must be returned in its original packaging, where applicable.

  • You must provide your order number or proof of purchase.

Items that do not meet these requirements may not be eligible for a refund.

2. How to Request a Return

To request a return, please contact us at:

Email: contact@driphope.com

Please include your order number and explain the reason for your return.

Do not send an item back before contacting us and receiving return instructions. Returns sent without prior authorization may not be accepted.

If your return request is approved, we will provide you with instructions regarding where and how to send your return.

Return address:

3. Return Shipping

Unless the item is defective, damaged, incorrect, or otherwise covered by applicable consumer protection law, the customer is responsible for return shipping costs.

If we determine that the return is due to an error on our part, such as receiving a defective or incorrect item, we will provide appropriate instructions for resolving the issue.

4. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your order as soon as you receive it.

If your item is damaged, defective, or you received the wrong item, please contact us at contact@driphope.com as soon as possible.

Please provide:

  • Your order number

  • A description of the problem

  • Clear photos of the item and, where relevant, the packaging

We will review the issue and determine the appropriate resolution, which may include a replacement, refund, or other appropriate solution.

5. Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following items are generally not eligible for return:

  • Items that have been worn or used

  • Items without their original tags

  • Items that are damaged after delivery due to misuse or improper care

  • Gift cards

  • Items clearly identified as final sale or non-returnable at the time of purchase

If you are unsure whether a particular item is eligible for return, please contact us before sending it back.

6. Sale and Promotional Items

Unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase, items purchased during a sale or promotional period may be eligible for return under the same conditions described in this policy.

If a specific product is marked Final Sale or Non-Returnable, it will not be eligible for return unless required by applicable law.

7. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges.

If you would like a different size, color, or product, the quickest option is to request a return for the original item.

Once the return has been approved and processed, you may place a new order for the item you want.

Please note that the desired replacement item may be subject to availability.

8. European Union — 14-Day Right of Withdrawal

If you are a consumer located in the European Union, you may have a legal right to cancel your order within 14 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason, subject to applicable EU consumer protection laws and any applicable exceptions.

This statutory right is separate from our voluntary 30-day return policy.

Where applicable, returned goods should be handled with reasonable care and returned in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Nothing in this policy is intended to limit or exclude any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you under the laws of your country.

9. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you whether your refund has been approved or rejected.

If your refund is approved, the refund will be issued to your original payment method.

We will generally process approved refunds within 10 business days.

Please note that after we process the refund, your bank, credit card company, or payment provider may require additional time to reflect the funds in your account.

If you have not received your refund after 15 business days from the date we approved it, please contact us at:

contact@driphope.com

10. Refund Amount

Approved refunds will generally cover the purchase price of the eligible returned item.

Original shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part, the item is defective, or a refund of those charges is otherwise required by applicable law.
